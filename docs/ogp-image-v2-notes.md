## 使用プロンプト

Use case: compositing. Edit image 1 (existing OGP) using image 2 as the typography and logo-lockup reference. Final landscape Open Graph banner intended 1200x630, 40:21 ratio. Keep the warm pale peach background from image 1 but make it flat and clean. Replace the huge decorative serif lettering with typography closely matching image 2: brown bold clean sans-serif with slightly spaced letters, NOT serif. Exact title on ONE line: "Whisky Log". Smaller Japanese subtitle below, exactly "ウイスキーログ", letterspaced bold Japanese Gothic sans-serif. Place a small circular pale amber badge with the original smiling whisky glass illustration to the left of the text, like image 2. Preserve the glass character identity and illustration style. Center the entire horizontal logo and text lockup on the canvas. At 1200px width, whole lockup approximately 630px wide and 170px tall; circular icon diameter 165px, gap 32px; English font about 58px and Japanese about 28px. Very generous empty space on every side. Both logo and text MUST be substantially smaller than image 1. No outside sparkles, no extra text, no border or watermark. Clean crisp restrained branded OGP.
